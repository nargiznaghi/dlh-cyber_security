# Clinical Downtime Quick-Reference Guide
**MedDefense Health Systems — Clinical Operations**  
**Classification:** Operational / Patient Safety  

---

## 1. Epic EHR Downtime Procedures

### Overview & Declaration
When electronic patient records become unavailable, patient care continues uninterrupted using pre-printed paper documentation. **The Nursing Supervisor, in consultation with the Administrator on Call (Extension 7000), formally declares Epic EHR downtime** when system unavailability exceeds 15 minutes or upon explicit instruction.

### Physical Location of Paper Forms
Paper downtime packets are stored in **Drawer 2 of the primary filing cabinet at each Nursing Station** (Nursing Station Alpha on Floor 2 North, Nursing Station Beta on Floor 3 South, and the Emergency Department Triage Desk). Packets contain:
* Paper Physician Order Sheets
* Paper Medication Administration Records (MAR)
* Manual Lab and Imaging Requisition Slips
* Patient Identification Logs

---

### Step-by-Step Downtime Instructions

1. **Activate Downtime Mode:** **Immediately notify all unit staff that Epic is offline.** Instruct all providers to transition to paper charting using forms retrieved from Drawer 2.
2. **Apply Patient Identification Labels:** Affix pre-printed patient addressograph labels (or handwrite legibly if labels are unavailable) with **Full Patient Legal Name, MRN, Date of Birth, and Room/Bed Number** on every single sheet of paper used for charting.
3. **Record All Verbal and Written Orders:** Physicians must write all new orders, medication changes, and treatment instructions on **Paper Physician Order Sheets**. Two clinicians must verbally verify and co-sign all high-risk or critical medication orders.
4. **Execute Manual Medication Administration:** Nurses must record all medication administrations directly on the **Paper MAR** at the exact time of delivery. **Never administer medication without documenting it on paper immediately.**
5. **Manage Stat and Urgent Labs:** For urgent diagnostic testing, complete manual paper lab requisition slips and attach a red "DOWNTIME STAT" sticker before hand-delivering specimens directly to the laboratory reception desk.
6. **Escalate Critical Communication:** For urgent clinical concerns or worsening patient status requiring physician intervention, **contact the provider via Vocera/overhead paging immediately** rather than relying on electronic messaging.

### Escalation Path
* **Primary Support:** Nursing Supervisor (Extension 7100)
* **IT Outage Reporting:** IT Helpdesk / Incident Commander (Extension 7200)
* **Hospital Switchboard:** Extension 7000

### Post-Restoration Reconciliation Steps
1. **Wait for IT All-Clear:** Do not resume electronic entry until the IT Incident Commander formally announces system restoration and data verification.
2. **Back-Enter Historical Data:** **Designated unit clerks and clinical staff must enter all paper-recorded orders, vital signs, and medication administrations back into Epic within 4 hours of system restoration.**
3. **Verify and Cross-Check:** Compare every paper entry against electronic records to ensure zero missing doses or unrecorded clinical assessments.
4. **Audit and File:** Sign and date all completed paper downtime records, stamp them with an "EHR DOWNTIME RECONCILED" mark, and **deliver the physical packet to Medical Records (Health Information Management) within 24 hours** for permanent archival scanning.

## 2. Laboratory Information System (LIS) Downtime Procedures

### Overview & Declaration
When the Laboratory Information System is unavailable, blood and specimen testing switches to manual offline collection and courier transport. **The Laboratory Supervisor or Lead Medical Technologist declares LIS downtime** immediately upon noticing analyzer interface failure or receiving system outage notification from IT.

### Physical Location of Paper Forms & Supplies
Manual LIS downtime kits, blank requisition pads, and emergency specimen labels are located in **Cabinet B of the Central Laboratory Reception Area and inside the Phlebotomy Mobile Carts**.

---

### Step-by-Step Downtime Instructions

1. **Switch to Manual Labeling:** **Stop printing barcode specimen labels immediately.** Retrieve blank paper requisition slips and adhesive handwritten specimen labels from Cabinet B.
2. **Handwrite Specimen Labels:** For every patient draw, **legibly handwrite the Patient Full Name, MRN, Date of Birth, Exact Date, Collection Time, and Phlebotomist Initials** directly onto the specimen collection tube and requisition form. Unlabeled or illegible tubes will be rejected for patient safety.
3. **Prioritize Critical and Stat Orders:** Sort physical requisitions into strict priority order: **(1) Stat / Emergency, (2) Timed Meds (e.g., peak/trough antibiotics), (3) Routine Morning Labs.**
4. **Perform Manual Testing & Result Logging:** Technologists perform assays using standalone analyzer modes or manual test kits. Record all raw instrument readings and calculated results onto the **Manual Lab Log Sheet**.
5. **Call Critical Results Immediately:** **If a critical lab value is identified (e.g., Potassium > 6.0 mEq/L or Troponin elevation), telephone the attending nurse or physician immediately.** Read back the value, record the name of the clinician receiving the result, and note the exact notification time on the paper log.
6. **Hand-Deliver Paper Results:** Courier physical paper result reports directly to the requesting nursing station or emergency triage desk. Do not leave unverified paper results unattended.

### Escalation Path
* **Laboratory Supervisor / Pathologist on Call:** Extension 7300
* **Nursing Supervisor:** Extension 7100
* **IT Helpdesk:** Extension 7200

### Post-Restoration Reconciliation Steps
1. **Verify LIS and Interface Stability:** Wait for IT confirmation that the LIS database has been successfully restored and verified (including integrity checks and critical record validation).
2. **Batch Enter Historical Lab Results:** **Laboratory technicians must manually enter all paper log sheet results into the restored LIS database**, cross-referencing every entry against original requisition slips.
3. **Reconcile Patient Identifiers:** Double-check that every back-entered result is correctly matched to the corresponding patient MRN in Epic and LIS.
4. **Archive Paper Logs:** File all manual lab log sheets and paper requisitions in the Laboratory Quality Assurance binder for mandatory regulatory review and HIPAA audit compliance.
