# Executive Incident Brief: MedDefense Security Event IR-2026-0420-01

**To:** Board of Directors & Chief Executive Officer  
**From:** Dr. Patricia Morales (Executive Sponsor) / Nargiz Naghiyeva (Incident Response Lead)  
**Date:** April 21, 2026 (07:00Z Briefing)  

---

## Situation Overview
On Sunday, April 20, 2026, at 19:43 UTC, MedDefense detected unauthorized external access to our clinical network originating from a spearphishing email delivered on Thursday, April 17. The unauthorized party gained access to four clinical workstations and the central clinical documentation file server.

## Impact & Scope
- **Patient Impact:** A total of 1,847 patient files containing clinical notes and laboratory results were accessed and copied from our document storage server during the incident window.
- **Clinical Operations:** Emergency Department and clinical care services remained operational throughout the incident by utilizing secondary workstation pools. Staged malicious software intended to disrupt systems was successfully intercepted and neutralized before it could execute.

## Containment & Response Actions
- All affected workstations and network connections associated with the external threat actor have been completely isolated and secured.
- Malicious background services and files have been entirely removed from all systems.
- Enterprise-wide administrative credentials and security access tokens have been forcibly reset to block further unauthorized access.
- Comprehensive digital forensic evidence has been preserved to support ongoing regulatory reviews.

## Regulatory Posture
Because patient records were accessed by an unauthorized external party without a low probability of compromise, this incident meets the threshold for a reportable privacy breach under HIPAA regulations, requiring formal notification to federal authorities and affected patients within sixty days.

The next formal briefing will be provided to executive leadership and legal counsel on Monday, April 21 at 14:00 UTC following completion of identity protection planning and regulatory filing preparations.
