# MedDefense Health Systems - Incident Response Capability

> *"Plans are nothing; planning is everything."* — Dwight D. Eisenhower

## 1. Overview & Purpose
MedDefense Health Systems operates three regional hospital sites, serving 2,000 staff members and safeguarding critical patient electronic health records (EHR). This repository houses the formalized, production-grade **Incident Response (IR) Capability**, designed by the Security Operations Center (SOC) under the authorization of the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). 

These artifacts bridge the gap between chaotic operational reality and standardized, repeatable processes. They are built for operators—ensuring that an on-call analyst at 3:00 AM has immediate, unambiguous guidance to protect patient safety, maintain clinical operations, and satisfy regulatory reporting obligations.
