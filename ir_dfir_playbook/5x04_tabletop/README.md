# The Tabletop

## Introduction
> "The more you sweat in training, the less you bleed in battle." — Norman Schwarzkopf

A tabletop exercise tests an organization's leadership under pressure when facts are incomplete and the clock is running. This project simulates a supply chain compromise affecting MedDefense's patient scheduling vendor (**Nexus Patient Scheduling**).

## Learning Objectives
- **Technical:** Design scenario injects and evaluation criteria that test specific organizational capabilities.
- **Conceptual:** Master facilitation neutrality and convert exercise observations into actionable findings.
- **Transversal:** Design for stress, write for other facilitators, and enforce ownership for every finding.
